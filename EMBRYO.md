# Embryo Slicer

Embryo Slicer is Embryo-Zero's fork of [PrusaSlicer](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer) (version_3.0.0-alpha14, commit e6c85c5). It does 3D-printing slicer.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
